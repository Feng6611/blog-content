#blog

If a man reaches 35 without settling on an ambition and having something modest to show for it, he is already dead.

I can push societal expectations back to 30—maybe 35 at most, I suppose. By then, my dad will be 65, gradually reaching an age where you leave things to fate. If I have a long-term partner, she won't be that young anymore either. And I still want to have a kid.

What society and family expect from you is stability: a stable job, a steady income, and a settled lifestyle. You can't be too erratic. Jumping around comes with risk, and that risk has to be shouldered together.

Unless you have parents to live off of, someone rich to latch onto, or a trend to ride. Under ordinary circumstances, all you can really do is keep exploring, building up as much inner confidence as you can before that time of death arrives. That confidence is simply knowing yourself well enough.

Amen. May we all live forever.
