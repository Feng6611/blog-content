No matter how unhappy I feel about how little I read, at least my "read" count on Douban (China's Goodreads) has finally crossed 100. Now that I'm trying to train my writing by starting with reading notes, it's time to set down some basic rules.

Some books I absolutely love—they spark deep inspiration and genuine resonance. This kind doesn't just need detailed notes; it calls for thematic reading and one or more full review essays. Like *The Fountainhead*, Xinjiang... 5🌟

Others are worth reading and offer real takeaways. They spark ideas for topics or offer insights in certain sections, but without the emotional charge. For these, I can take the core question and write a note using the Feynman technique. Like *Alternative Facts*. 4🌟

Some are readable enough—decent books, but they don't offer much inspiration for where I am right now. 3🌟

The rest aren't worth reading at all.

So from the books I've read and how I've rated them, it should be pretty clear which areas I'm into and how much I've invested in them.

Just write first, then see if I can stretch it into something longer. Every 4-star and 5-star book should be an attempt at a full post. For me, they either pack an emotional punch or at least deliver concrete takeaways. 3-star books still count as readable for me, but I have zero interest in turning them into long posts—a short review is plenty. 1-star and 2-star books are simply unreadable.
